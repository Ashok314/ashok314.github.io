backstory: |
  This began in *Science Communication*, a course from my master's studies taught by Professor Gary Quinn. Our final class was held online during the COVID-19 pandemic.

  The course was about more than explaining scientific work. We also talked about bigger questions: What makes a good life? How do we know what we know? What shapes our choices? How should we face problems, mistakes, and our own limits? In the final class, Professor Quinn brought these ideas together in two questions. We had to finish these sentences:

  1. “When I am wise ...”
  2. “When I discover that I made an error, I react to this by ...”

  Everyone was given five to ten minutes to prepare. I did not set out to write poetry; both answers simply arrived that way. I wrote the two poems almost immediately and presented them to the class.

  The response was warm, and Professor Quinn asked me to email the poems to him. I sent them after class with a short thank-you note. The next day, he replied with encouragement and one line that has stayed with me: “I hope you will keep up your writing alongside your lab work.”

  What began as a quick classroom exercise became a small reminder that sometimes writing appears before we have time to overthink it—and that a few sincere words of encouragement can travel with us for years.

When I am wise ...
When I am wise, I would have a dream
When I am wise, I would have a plan
Rather keeping quiet, I would scream
I would transit from young boy to a man

When I am wise, I would seek light
When I am wise, I would have a life
Moving up from ground to new height
Work on my skills like a sharp knife

When I am wise, I would be myself
When I am wise, I would learn more
Open my arms for people to help
And invest to discover my inner core

When I am wise, I would never say goodbye
Until we meet again, see you when you come by

When I discover that I made an error I react to this by...

imperfections are part of life
they may be out of your five
just learn to improve one
rose is still beautiful with thorn

never fear to make a mistake
after which just don’t forget to wake
learning is the dependent process
acknowledge them to get success

errors are the traces of completeness
correct them with great willingness
don’t panic just decide right
bright day follows the dark night
